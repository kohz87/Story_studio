# Lanternfall (a tiny example world)

This is the whole lore for the example. It is one page on purpose: `pack.json` next to it is what the kit's prompts
produce from a page like this one. Write yours the same way: plain facts and names, nothing about numbers.

## The place

Lanternfall is a market town on the Slow River. Every street has lamps, and the lamps are never allowed to go out:
after dark the **Gloaming**, a creeping shadow that hates light, comes in from the marshes and drowns whatever is not
lit. Money is the **mark**.

- **Lanternfall**, the town. A lamp stall at the market sells bread, lamp oil, reed tonic and a few good tools.
- **Reedmoor**, the reed marsh south-east of town. Wisps drift there at dusk, and mud crawlers lie under the shallows.
- **The Lamp Tower**, north-west of town: the Lamplighters' guildhall and the highest beacon on the river.

## The Lamplighters

The Lamplighters' Guild keeps the lamps burning and the night roads open. A young **Lamplighter** carries a lantern and a pole. With years she is posted to a beacon tower as a **Beacon Warden**,
then stands the long watch as a **Night Keeper**, and the rare one is named **Keeper of the First Light** and carries the one
flame that has never gone out. Instructor Wick at the guildhall teaches the first lessons for a small fee.

Their arts: swinging the heavy lantern, throwing a spark from the wick, a glow that turns the first blow aside; later,
flares that dazzle a crowd, the lamp-hook that drags a foe off balance, a wall of lanterns, holy light that tears the
Gloaming; at the end a lance of dawn, a hymn that mends and keeps mending, and the long vigil that wears the dark down.

## Things and creatures

Trail bread, lamp oil, marsh reed, reed tonic (bitter, green, quick) and gloam ash, the grey ash a shadow leaves when it
burns out. A lamplighter's pole, an oilskin coat, and the tin ward charm the guild blesses.

Reed wisps are small and clever. Mud crawlers are slow and strong. The gloam hound runs the night roads and is the
first real danger.

## Work

The town pays a few marks to anyone who thins the wisps on the reed road. The guild pays more for gloam ash, and a
ward charm for the one who brings enough of it to the Lamp Tower.
